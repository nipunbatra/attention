A three-part series for deep-learning students

Next-token prediction, attention and the Transformer

A planned sequence from character prediction to a trainable Transformer. Part 2 is available now: how attention lets a token read information from its context.

Part 2 is both a classroom presentation and a self-study article. Its numbers come from a tiny, hand-designed model with checkable arithmetic, and the complete lesson works offline as one file.

    From characters to next-token prediction

    What you will buildA character model for names: a lookup table, a fixed window of embeddings, one hidden layer and a softmax, trained on real names and sampled with a temperature.

    For students who know MLPs, softmax and cross-entropy.

Reading and presenting. Every page opens as an article. The strip at the top switches between the full text and a lean view for projection, and the Present button (or P) turns the page into frames: one frame fills the screen and each → reveals the next step. O shows all frames, S shows the notes, and Presenter view opens the notes and the clock in a second window.
