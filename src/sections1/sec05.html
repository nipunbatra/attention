Concatenate the embeddings

We have looked up one embedding row for each character in the window. The MLP expects one row, so we put the three embeddings side by side. Three positions with two coordinates each give us six numbers. The first two always describe the oldest character, and the last two describe the newest. Reversing the characters changes where their numbers appear in this row. Adding the embeddings would lose that order because addition is commutative.
