Characters are categorical symbols. An MLP needs rows of numbers.

Characters to numbers

A one-hot vector identifies a character with one active coordinate. Any two different characters have zero dot product, so this geometry cannot express degrees of similarity. An embedding table gives each character a learned row of two numbers instead of a long one-hot vector. A lookup just selects that row; there is no averaging or arithmetic to do. During training, we constrained the first axis so vowels are positive and consonants are negative. The second axis was free to move wherever it helped lower the name-prediction loss. These labels make our toy table easier to read. Learned coordinates in larger models rarely have such simple names.
