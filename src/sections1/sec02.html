Why we are here: a small character vocabulary lets us inspect every input, score, and probability.

Generate a name

We will train a network on Indian given names. Each letter is one token, and the boundary symbol marks both the empty space before a name and the stopping point after it. That gives the model a vocabulary of the boundary plus the alphabet. At each step it receives three character IDs and returns a probability for every possible next character. The displayed distribution for aab comes from the trained model bundled with this page. Its answer need not be certain. It only has to place useful probability on characters that often continue similar contexts.

One boundary and the alphabet

The vocabulary is $\mathcal V=\{\text{-},a,\ldots,z\}$, with one output class per token.
