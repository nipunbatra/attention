Self-attention connects allowed tokens directly. What does that gain, and what does it cost?

Sequence models side by side

A fixed-window MLP sees only the tokens in its window. An RNN can carry information over longer sequences, but distant tokens communicate through many recurrent updates. Temporal convolutions reach more positions as we add layers. Self-attention connects any allowed pair directly within one layer and computes positions in parallel during training. It also needs a score for every allowed query-key pair, so the number of score cells grows quadratically with sequence length. These are trade-offs, not a ranking. Memory, latency, data, and the task all affect which model we choose.
