Why we are here: flexible context and short information paths make self-attention useful, though its score matrix has a cost.

Sequence models side by side

A fixed-window MLP sees only the tokens placed in its window. An RNN can carry information over longer sequences, but the route between distant tokens passes through many recurrent updates. Temporal convolutions enlarge their receptive field as layers accumulate. Self-attention gives any allowed pair a direct path inside one layer and computes positions in parallel during training. That flexibility requires a score for every allowed query-key pair, so a full sequence uses a quadratic number of score cells. The table compares these properties without declaring one model best for every setting. Memory, latency, data, and task structure all affect the choice.
