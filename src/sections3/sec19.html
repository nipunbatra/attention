Why we are here: the series closes by reducing learning, the block, and generation to one compact statement each.

Three summaries

The lesson has moved through three scales. At the training-example scale, the observed next token gives a loss, reverse-mode autodiff supplies gradients, and SGD changes every contributing parameter. At the block scale, LayerNorm prepares residual rows, multi-head attention mixes information across allowed positions, and an FFN mixes features within each position. At the language-model scale, token and position rows pass through many causal blocks, the vocabulary head supplies next-token probabilities, and generation appends one token at a time while reusing cached keys and values. The three cards keep those scales separate while showing how they connect.

Learning

Observed target $\to$ cross-entropy $\to$ gradients $\to$ one parameter update.

One block

Pre-norm causal multi-head attention and a pre-norm FFN each add an update to the residual stream.

The model

Embeddings plus positions $\to$ stacked causal blocks $\to$ vocabulary logits $\to$ repeated generation.
