Which numbers will the optimizer change?

The token and position tables, projection matrices, and vocabulary head are parameters. Training changes their values and saves them. The rows $\ve{e}$, $\vq{q}$, $\vk{k}$, $\vv{v}$, scores, attention weights, messages, updates, logits, and probabilities are intermediates: we compute them again for each new sequence. Autograd keeps enough of these temporary values to calculate gradients, and the optimizer uses the gradients to update the parameters. These are two different kinds of update. $\vd{\Delta e}$ changes a representation during the forward pass; an optimizer step changes the stored weights used by future forward passes.
