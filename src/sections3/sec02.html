Why we are here: a prediction becomes a training signal only when the next observed token is known.

The observed next token tells us the error

The river prefix ends with the word the, and the model assigns one probability to every vocabulary token. Training then reveals the observed continuation, water. Cross-entropy keeps the probability placed on that target and turns it into a loss with a negative logarithm. A high target probability gives a small loss. A low target probability gives a large loss. The other probabilities still matter because softmax couples every logit through one denominator. Open the worksheet to see that denominator, then follow the target row into the loss calculation. Both values come from the same forward pass used in Part 2.
