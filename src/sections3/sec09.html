The residual stream and LayerNorm

The residual stream is the model-width row carried through the stack. Attention and the FFN read it, compute updates, and add those updates back. LayerNorm works within one row: subtract the row mean, then divide by $\sqrt{\operatorname{var}+\epsilon}$. The small $\epsilon$ keeps the denominator nonzero even when every value in the row is the same. Our pre-norm decoder applies LayerNorm before attention and before the FFN. Move the sliders to see the mean, centered values, variance, and normalized values change. The calculator uses $\epsilon=10^{-5}$ and fixes the scale and shift at $\gamma=1$, $\beta=0$. A trainable LayerNorm normally learns its scale and shift. See the PyTorch LayerNorm definition.
