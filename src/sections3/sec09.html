The residual stream and LayerNorm

The residual stream is the model-width row carried through the stack. Attention and the FFN read from that stream, compute updates, and add those updates back. LayerNorm acts within one row: it subtracts the row mean and divides by $\sqrt{\operatorname{var}+\epsilon}$. The small $\epsilon$ keeps the denominator nonzero, even for a constant row. In the pre-norm decoder used here, LayerNorm sits before attention and before the FFN. The sliders let you inspect the mean, centered values, variance, and normalized values. This calculator uses $\epsilon=10^{-5}$ and fixes the learned scale and shift to $\gamma=1$, $\beta=0$; a trainable LayerNorm normally learns them. See the PyTorch LayerNorm definition.
