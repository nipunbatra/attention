Where Part 2 left us

In Part 2, we followed one causal attention head from a token representation to a next-token distribution. The same bank row changed toward water in the river sentence and toward finance in the cheque sentence. The coordinates below come from that hand-written toy, with its named representation, query, key, and value axes. We chose the weights to make the calculation easy to follow, but the arithmetic is real. We will first reuse those weights, then compare the prediction with the observed word water. That error lets us change the parameters that contributed to the prediction: the forward pass we already know becomes a training example.

A half-right guess is the signal that tells hundreds of stored numbers which way to move. This part: learning, then what a full Transformer stacks on top.

Attention reads earlier rows, builds $\vd{\Delta e_i}$, and adds it to $\ve{e_i}$. The output head turns the final updated row into next-token probabilities.
