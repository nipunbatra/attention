Why we are here: embeddings, blocks, and the vocabulary head now form one decoder-only language model.

The complete decoder-only model

A decoder-only Transformer begins with token embeddings plus positions, sends the resulting rows through a stack of causal blocks, and applies a vocabulary head to each final row. Every block preserves model width, so residual additions remain possible throughout the stack. The vocabulary head maps one final five-feature row to one logit per token in this toy vocabulary. Softmax turns those logits into probabilities. During training, every position can contribute a loss. During generation, only the distribution at the last position is needed to choose the next token.
