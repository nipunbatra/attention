The numerical toy has the same width for embeddings, queries, keys and values, but their roles and projection matrices differ. Equal shape does not mean equal numbers or interchangeable meaning. The frozen image encoder's width is two; only its connector output has the decoder width of three.

The contribution chart is exact bookkeeping for this single attention block and linear vocabulary head. Let $u_{\text{two}}$ and $u_{\text{one}}$ be the corresponding columns of $W_{\mathrm{vocab}}$. Then $z_{\text{two}}-z_{\text{one}}=e_7(u_{\text{two}}-u_{\text{one}})+b_{\text{two}}-b_{\text{one}}+\sum_j\alpha_{7j}v_jW_O(u_{\text{two}}-u_{\text{one}})$. Grouping the sum into image and text terms gives the chart. These terms describe the current forward pass; they are not causal effects of deleting raw pixels. Deleting or replacing a source would also change the attention weights.

Values and contributions use full precision internally and three decimals in the tables. The next section keeps the image fixed while the growing answer supplies new last-token queries.
