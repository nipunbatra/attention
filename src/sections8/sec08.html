Only the answer tokens carry a target. The image and the question are context, in the loss as in the forward pass.

Train on the answer

Training uses teacher forcing: the correct answer is placed after the question, the mask keeps each row from seeing later tokens, and only the answer rows are scored. Here that is two targets: the question mark should predict two, and the two row should predict the end marker. The run below fits scenes A and B in 73 Adam steps; the overlay of what the question mark reads goes from an even smear to the four mug patches. Then one more step on scene A alone: its loss falls, scene B's loss rises. Keep that failure; it is the reason for batches and held-out checks.

loss = F.cross_entropy(logits[answer_rows], answer_ids)   # rows 21 and 22 only
The loss is over the vocabulary softmax of two rows. Nothing about attention weights appears in it directly; they change only because they change those two rows.

optimizer.zero_grad()
loss.backward()      # gradients for the connector, the text table, the head, the block
optimizer.step()     # the frozen encoder is not in the optimizer

Two answer rows carry the whole loss. Fitting them moved the question mark's reading onto the mugs; one further step on a single scene showed how easily that balance tips.
