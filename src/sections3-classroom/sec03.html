TinyStories: text becomes training examples

Our subset has 6,000 short, synthetic stories. Split whole stories before making next-token windows.

TinyStories by Ronen Eldan and Yuanzhi Li, CDLA-Sharing-1.0. This is a 6,000-document subset, not the complete dataset. The saved split audit reports zero document overlap. Read a complete source story and its provenance.

PAD: padding · BOS: beginning of story · EOS: end of story · UNK: unknown token.

The vocabulary is fitted on training stories only. The tokenizer separates punctuation too; “word” is shorthand for a vocabulary token. BOS/EOS wrap a complete story, not each sentence. Left-pad short prefixes, crop long prefixes to their last 64 tokens, and never cross a story boundary.

Slide the boundary one token forward to make another example. Each example still occupies 64 slots.
