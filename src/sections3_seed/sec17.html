Why we are here: one head is one notion of relevance, and a token usually needs several at once.

Multiple heads

One head produces one weight row per token, so it can only express one kind of "who matters to me right now". The word bank needs more than that: the meaning from river, the phrase it sits in, and the subject of the sentence. Multi-head attention runs several small heads side by side, each with its own $W_Q, W_K, W_V$, and lets $W_O$ fold their messages into one update.

Pick a head in the table below and follow it through the diagram: its own projections, its own weight row, its own block of $H$. The sentence to remember: heads differ only in their weight matrices; inside each one, attention is exactly section 16.

One attention head computes one weight row per token: one answer to "who is relevant to me?". But there may be multiple useful notions of relevance. For bank, one head might want the semantic context (river), another the words right before it, another the start of the sentence.

The equations, head by head

$$\vq{Q^{(h)}} = \ve{E}\,W_Q^{(h)}, \qquad \vk{K^{(h)}} = \ve{E}\,W_K^{(h)}, \qquad \vv{V^{(h)}} = \ve{E}\,W_V^{(h)}$$
$$H^{(h)} = \operatorname{Attention}\!\left(\vq{Q^{(h)}}, \vk{K^{(h)}}, \vv{V^{(h)}}\right)$$
$$H = \left[H^{(1)};\,\ldots;\,H^{(n_h)}\right]$$
$$\boxed{\vd{\Delta E} = H\,W_O}, \qquad \vp{E'} = \ve{E} + \vd{\Delta E}$$

Inside each head, Attention is exactly the single-head computation of section 16: scale, mask, softmax, then $\va{A^{(h)}}\,\vv{V^{(h)}}$. The heads only differ in their weight matrices.

Heads do not make the model wider. Every head reads the full input $\ve{e_i}$, all $d_{\text{model}}$ numbers, through its own $W_Q^{(h)}, W_K^{(h)}, W_V^{(h)}$; no head receives a slice of the embedding. What is smaller is each head's output, $d_v = d_{\text{model}} / n_h$ numbers per token, and those outputs sit side by side in $H$.

Keep in mind: a head is not a slice of the input. Every head sees all of $\ve{e_i}$, computes its own weights, and returns a short message; $W_O$ is where those messages meet and become one $\vd{\Delta e_i}$.
