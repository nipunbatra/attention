CLS participates in the attention blocks, so it can gather a content-dependent summary at every layer. Mean pooling combines contextual patch rows at the end. Both are viable design choices. Our pretrained checkpoint and full small model use CLS, so we follow it consistently through the worked examples. Neither option is guaranteed to be best for every dataset.
