Sixty steps on the sphere

The loss connects to every parameter: the twelve entries of the image map, the rows of the words in the captions, and the log scale. Autograd differentiates the whole path, including the division by each vector's length, and the optimiser takes one step. We run 60 steps of plain gradient descent with learning rate 0.05, all three pairs at once, and record the six unit vectors after every step. Drag the slider or press Play. Observed pairs pull together, wrong pairs push apart, and the temperature drops from 0.50 to 0.21, which sharpens every row. The words learn too: a moves away from mug because it never appeared with the mugs.
