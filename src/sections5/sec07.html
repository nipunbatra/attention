CLS holds a row that depends on the scene. A class head turns it into one probability per answer, and a label scores it.

From the CLS row to an answer

The updated CLS row has four coordinates on named axes; it is a representation, not yet an answer. A class head is a small matrix with one column per answer and a bias for each: $\ell = \vp{e_0'} W_{\text{cls}} + b_{\text{cls}}$. Softmax over the two logits gives two probabilities, and the loss is minus the log of the probability of the true label, exactly as in the text parts. Work the two logits by hand, then run scene B through the same untrained model. It says “mug on the right” for both scenes, which is wrong for B. That wrong answer is the reason for the next section.

Two answers, so two logits: $\ell = \vp{e_0'}\,W_{\text{cls}} + b_{\text{cls}}$, with $W_{\text{cls}}$ of shape $4\times 2$.

Two softmaxes on this page: one over seventeen source rows (where CLS reads), one over two answers (what the model says). They share a formula and nothing else.

Scene A, label: 

logits = E_new[0] @ W_cls + b_cls          # [2]
loss = F.cross_entropy(logits[None, :], torch.tensor([label]))

The forward pass is complete: pixels to rows, rows to a routed message, a message to an updated CLS row, the row to two probabilities, and a label to score them. The untrained model is wrong on scene B. Next we change its numbers.
