Turn the image representation into a prediction

Our question was whether the image contains one or two occupied 2×2 blocks. Attention has collected information in the CLS row, but those two coordinates are still features. A classification head converts them into one score per answer. We will calculate both scores, normalize them, and check the probability given to the observed label. The initial weights are the same hand-chosen values used throughout this calculation.

The head makes two scores: one for two blocks, one for one block.

Use the two blocks column of $W_{\mathrm{class}}$. Its weights are $(0.5,0)$; its bias is $0$.

This score is a logit. It can be any real number; it is not a probability yet.

Keep the same CLS row. The one block column has weights $(0,0.25)$ and bias $0$.

# CLS: [2]; W_class: [2, 2]; bias: [2]
logits = E_next[0] @ W_class + b_class

These two probabilities compete over output labels. The five attention weights competed over input rows.

The observed label for our image is two blocks.

target = torch.tensor([0])  # two blocks
loss = F.cross_entropy(logits[None, :], target)
