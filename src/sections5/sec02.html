The scene: one picture, one question

Part 2 had one sentence and one blank. This page has one scene and one question: is there a mug on the right half of the table? The photograph shows why the question is easy for you and hard for a grid of pixels: the mugs sit wherever they sit, and a patch grid cuts straight through them. We cannot multiply a photograph by hand, so the arithmetic runs on an 8×8 version of the same tabletop, with four grey levels and named regions. Read the pixel values once; from here on, every table on this page is about these sixteen patches, and every attention map is painted on this grid.
