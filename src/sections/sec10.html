“bank”: same start, different context

So far the page has followed one sentence. This section runs the same computation twice, once for the river sentence and once for the cheque sentence, and watches the word bank. Its starting row is identical in both, because the token and the position are the same. Compare the $\va{\alpha_{7j}}$ columns first: river carries most of the weight in sentence A, cheque and deposited in sentence B. Then read down to the footer and to the $\vd{\Delta e}$ row, where the difference lands: on the water axis in A, on the finance axis in B. Drag the slider to add the update a little at a time. The sentence to remember: the same starting representation receives different information and becomes context-dependent.

Both sentences put bank at position 7. Its starting representation $\ve{e_{\text{bank}}^{(0)}}$ is the same row in both: same token embedding, same position. Attention is causal, so bank reads itself and the tokens to its left (tokens 1 to 7). The words after it play no part.
