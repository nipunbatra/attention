Full walkthrough, one step at a time

Everything before this section showed one stage at a time. Here the whole computation runs end to end on ten tokens, with the same numbers you would get by hand. Each stage is a small spreadsheet: one row per token, one column per coordinate, the result in the last column or the footer row. Click a score, or press Show the arithmetic, to see the numbers that produced it. At step 3, pick a different query token and press Next step again; the scores, the mask and the weights all change, but the recipe does not. The sentence to remember: one query, one score per earlier token, one weighted sum, one update added to the token.

Ten tokens, one query, eighteen steps. Press Next step and watch each stage produce its numbers. The pipeline shows where you are in the chain $\ve{e} \to \vq{Q},\vk{K},\vv{V} \to \text{attention} \to \vd{\Delta e} \to \vp{e + \Delta e}$.
