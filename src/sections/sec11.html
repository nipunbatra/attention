Why we are here: a common confusion. Are queries, keys and values three new embeddings? No. This section says what they are instead.

What exactly are Q, K, and V?

Students often hear query, key and value and picture three new embeddings. This section exists to stop that. Pick any token in the chips and read the four rows: the three lower rows are computed from the single $\ve{e}$ row above them by three fixed matrices, and nothing else about the token changes. The axis names say what each row means: a query row is what the token asks for, a key row is what it offers, a value row is what it sends. Below them sit the three matrices themselves, shared by every token. Step through the lifetime strip to see when each row exists inside one attention layer. The sentence to remember: $\vq{q}$, $\vk{k}$ and $\vv{v}$ are working vectors made from $\ve{e}$, and $\ve{e} + \vd{\Delta e}$ is the only thing that moves on.

Take the current representation of one token, $\ve{e_i}$. Three fixed matrices read it and produce three short rows. They are working vectors computed from $\ve{e_i}$; none of them replaces it.

$$\ve{e_i} \;\rightarrow\; \begin{cases} \vq{q_i} = \ve{e_i}\,W_Q \\[2pt] \vk{k_i} = \ve{e_i}\,W_K \\[2pt] \vv{v_i} = \ve{e_i}\,W_V \end{cases}$$

One token, three projections

Pick a token. The four numbers in the top row are the only input; each lower row is that row times one 4×3 matrix. Read the axis names above the cells.

Lifetime inside one attention layer

Step through the layer and watch which rows exist at each moment: $\ve{e_i}$ persists, $\vq{q_i}, \vk{k_i}, \vv{v_i}$ are computed and used, $\vd{\Delta e_i}$ is added to $\ve{e_i}$.

The one chain, again

The three projections sit in the second box; they are inputs to attention, and only $\vd{\Delta e}$ comes out of it.

Keep in mind: the token representation is $\ve{e}$, before and after. $\vq{q}$, $\vk{k}$ and $\vv{v}$ were made from it and used, and $\vd{\Delta e}$ is what came back.
