Why we are here: this is next-token prediction, so a token may read the past and never the future.

Causal masking

The model is trained to predict the next token, so during training the answer for position $i$ sits right there at position $i+1$. This section shows what stops a token from reading it. Switch the causal mask off and watch row 5: with the mask on, token 5 (the) reads only tokens 1 to 5; with it off, most of its weight lands on river and bank, the words it is supposed to predict. Press the send button in both states and compare the message that arrives. The sentence to remember: token $i$ may read from $j \le i$ and never from $j > i$, and the mask enforces that with a $-\infty$ before the softmax.

So far one query token opened the records to its left and we simply left the later ones closed. That was a habit. This section turns it into a rule and shows where the rule comes from: the training objective itself.

Every position predicts its next token at the same time

Training does not run one query at a time. One pass through the sentence scores every position at once: each token is a query, reads the tokens up to itself, and is graded on the token that follows it.

Short sequence shown; the ten-token sentence continues the same staircase. Position $i$ may read tokens $1 \ldots i$ and must predict token $i+1$.

Every query token $i$ compares against every key $j$, giving the score matrix $S = QK^\top/\sqrt{d_k}$ with entries $s_{ij}$. Token $i$ may retrieve only from $j \le i$, never from $j > i$. A causal mask sets the forbidden scores to $-\infty$ before the softmax, so their weights become exactly 0.

Allowed and masked

$$\begin{bmatrix} \checkmark & \times & \times & \times\\ \checkmark & \checkmark & \times & \times\\ \checkmark & \checkmark & \checkmark & \times\\ \checkmark & \checkmark & \checkmark & \checkmark \end{bmatrix}$$

Row $i$ = query token, column $j$ = key token. $\checkmark$: allowed ($j \le i$). $\times$: masked ($j > i$).

As a mask matrix

Adding $-\infty$ before the softmax is the same as deleting that column for that row: $e^{-\infty} = 0$.

Keep in mind: with the mask off the rows still sum to 1, so the leak is invisible in the totals and visible only in where the weight went. The mask is not a detail of one example; it is what makes next-token training honest.
