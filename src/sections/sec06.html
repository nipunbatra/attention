Hard retrieval → soft retrieval

A search engine picks one winner and shows you that page. Attention keeps the whole ranked list and blends the values in proportion to how well each key matched. This section makes that blend a spreadsheet: the scores from the previous section feed a softmax, the weights fill the $\va{\alpha_j}$ column, every value row is scaled by its weight, and the footer adds the rows up. Start in hard mode, where one row carries all the weight, then switch to soft and lower the temperature to watch the two modes meet. Drag a score up and watch every weight move. The sentence to remember: attention returns a weighted mixture of values, not one value.

A normal search engine keeps only the winner. It picks the key with the largest score and returns that item's value:

$$j^* = \arg\max_j\, \vq{q}^\top \vk{k_j}, \qquad \text{return } \vv{v_{j^*}}$$

Attention instead turns the scores into weights that sum to 1, and returns a blend of all the values, in proportion to those weights:

$$\va{\alpha_j} = \operatorname{softmax}_j\!\left(\vq{q}^\top \vk{k_j}\right), \qquad \boxed{\sum_j \va{\alpha_j}\, \vv{v_j}}$$

Scores: the query row against the six key rows, as before. Drag a slider to change a score

Look at the score column: it is the only thing the next table reads. A score you drag no longer equals the dot product of its row, so think of it as a different key.

Retrieve: weights from the scores, then each value row scaled by its weight, then the sum

In soft mode the weights are $\va{\alpha_j} = \operatorname{softmax}_j(s_j/\tau)$. A small $\tau$ makes the mixture sharper. As $\tau \to 0$, soft retrieval becomes hard retrieval when one score is strictly the largest; tied top scores keep sharing the weight. $\tau = 1$ is the formula above.

Attention does not necessarily retrieve one thing. It retrieves a weighted mixture of information.

Each piece now has a job. A Query says what the searcher needs, Keys decide who matches, Values carry what comes back, and softmax turns scores into a mixture. Back to language.

Keep in mind: the scores decide the weights, the weights decide the mixture, and a change in one score moves every weight at once, because the softmax keeps them summing to 1.
