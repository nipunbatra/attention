Why we are here: a contextual representation is only useful if it changes the prediction.

From Δe to the next-token probabilities

Sections 09 to 13 built an updated vector for one token. This section closes the loop: the updated last token is what the output head reads, so the earlier words can only reach the prediction through that one update. Switch the context between the river sentence and the cheque sentence and follow the three tables from top to bottom: the attention row moves, the update row changes, the probability row changes. Then switch attention off and switch the context again; nothing moves, exactly as in section 02. The sentence to remember: earlier tokens change the prediction only through the update they send to the last token.

Do not stop at $\vp{e_t'}$. The updated last token goes straight into the output head from section 02, now fed with context:

$$\ell = \vp{e_t'}\,W_{\text{vocab}} + b, \qquad p(x_{t+1}\mid x_{\le t}) = \operatorname{softmax}(\ell)$$
