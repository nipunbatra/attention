What this example has, and what it does not yet have

  • One attention head computes a contextual update; residual addition forms $e_i' = e_i + \Delta e_i$.
  • The vocabulary head reads the final updated row to give next-token probabilities.
  • Generation repeats that prediction with a growing prefix and fixed parameters.
  • Training updates parameters; stacked layers apply further transformations. Those are different operations.
