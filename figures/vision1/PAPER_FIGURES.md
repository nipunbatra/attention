# Original architecture figures in the opening

The two opening slides reproduce the authors' diagrams with visible source
credits. Colored outlines and accompanying explanations belong to this lecture.
The original PNG files are unmodified.

- **Text Transformer:** Vaswani et al., *Attention Is All You Need* (2017),
  Figure 1. Reuses `../transformer-paper/architecture.png`; provenance and the
  paper's figure-reproduction permission are recorded in
  `../transformer-paper/README.md`.
- **Vision Transformer:** Dosovitskiy et al., *An Image is Worth 16x16 Words:
  Transformers for Image Recognition at Scale* (2020; ICLR 2021), Figure 1.
  `vit-paper-architecture.png` is the original 1327 × 670 PNG retrieved on
  2026-09-29 from https://arxiv.org/html/2010.11929v2/model_scheme.png.
  Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.11929. Reproduced for attributed educational
  discussion; copyright remains with the original rights holders.

The source figures show the original papers' architectures. The lecture's
smaller pretrained model has its own dimensions, as explained in the later
walkthrough. The ViT figure's nine drawn patches are schematic; “16×16” denotes
the pixel dimensions of a patch in a /16 model.
