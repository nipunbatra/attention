# Transformer paper images

Source: Ashish Vaswani et al., *Attention Is All You Need* (2017), [arXiv:1706.03762](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762).

- `first-page.png`: complete first page rendered from the [arXiv v7 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1706.03762v7), retrieved 2026-09-15. The visible 2023 date identifies the revision, not the paper's first publication. Rendered with Poppler: `pdftoppm -f 1 -singlefile -scale-to 1800 -png attention-is-all-you-need.pdf first-page`.
- `architecture.png`: unmodified [Figure 1 image from the arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1706.03762v7/Figures/ModalNet-21.png), retrieved 2026-09-15. This is the authors' original encoder-decoder diagram, not a diagram of the lecture's smaller teaching model.

Both appear with author/source attribution in Lecture 2 and open at full resolution. The paper explicitly permits reproduction of its tables and figures in journalistic or scholarly works with proper attribution. The title-page screenshot identifies and discusses the paper in this educational lecture. The downloaded PDF is a temporary rendering input and is not redistributed in this directory.
